06 / APPLIANCE RESOURCE CENTER

QUESTIONS?
LET'S FIND ANSWERS.

Quick, practical answers for common appliance problems. This is general guidance, not a substitute for a qualified diagnosis.

14 questions

?My washer fills but won’t spin. What should I check?+
Try redistributing an uneven load and confirm the lid or door latches properly. If it still won't spin, stop and request a diagnosis; do not bypass any safety switches.
?Why is my washer leaking?+
Stop the cycle if water is escaping. Check the visible supply-hose connections and the drain hose for loose fittings without reaching near wet electrical connections. Persistent leaking needs service.
?Why is my washer shaking so much?+
An unbalanced load or an uneven floor can cause vibration. Try a smaller, balanced load and check whether the machine sits level. Heavy banging that continues may indicate a mechanical problem.
?Why does my dryer take so long to dry clothes?+
Clean the lint screen before each load and check whether the outside vent flap opens freely during operation. Restricted airflow can increase drying time and create a fire hazard. Have the vent system inspected if airflow is poor.
?My dryer smells hot or burnt. What should I do?+
Stop using it immediately. Turn it off if safe to do so and have it inspected before running it again. If there is smoke or fire, leave the area and call emergency services.
?What is included in dryer cleaning or maintenance?+
The flyer advertises cleaning out the dryer and greasing rollers. Ask which components are included for your specific model and whether the vent duct or other parts require a separate service.
?Why is water standing in my dishwasher?+
With the dishwasher off, check for food debris in the accessible filter and make sure the sink or disposal drains normally. Avoid opening electrical panels. If water remains, arrange service.
?Why are my dishes coming out dirty?+
Confirm the spray arms can turn, detergent is fresh, and dishes are loaded without blocking water flow. Clean the user-removable filter according to your owner's manual.
?My oven isn’t heating evenly. What could cause that?+
Check that the correct cooking mode and rack position are selected. An inaccurate temperature sensor, damaged element, or other issue may require testing. Do not work on internal gas or high-voltage parts yourself.
?My gas stove smells like gas. What should I do?+
Leave the area immediately. Do not operate switches, flames, appliances, or phones near the suspected leak. From a safe location, contact the gas utility or emergency services. Don't attempt a repair.
?Where do I find my appliance model and serial number?+
Look for a manufacturer label around the door opening, door frame, rear panel, or under the lid, depending on the appliance. You can send a clear photo of the label when requesting service.
?Can you tell me what my appliance error code means?+
Error codes vary by manufacturer and model. Send us the appliance brand, full model number, exact code, and what happened before it appeared. We can discuss an appropriate next step.
?Do you cover my town? How do pricing and appointments work?+
The service area advertised is Baldwin County and the Escambia County area. The flyer lists a $100 trip charge and an $85 labor-only promotion separately. Contact the business to confirm your address, current prices, whether charges are combined, parts costs, and available appointment times.
?How should I prepare for a repair visit?+
Keep the appliance accessible, note the symptoms and error codes, and find the model number. Keep pets and children away from the work area. Do not keep operating a machine that leaks, sparks, smokes, or smells like gas.
